A **fold** is a shimmering boundary around a scroll area. It's the scroll viewport's footprint extended into a column from the owner's floor up through the [[Screen]]. Machines that belong to the scroll area are only drawn inside it. A row that scrolls out of view doesn't fall off an edge or stack up somewhere; it slides into the fold and is gone, and slides back out when you scroll back.

That's the "higher-dimensional" part: content beyond the scroll area still exists (React still renders it), it just isn't drawn in this space.

## What a fold clips

Only things that belong to the scroll area's content: list items' casings, their pipes and links, and the piston heads. The scroll owner's own casing stays put, half inside the fold, with the piston rod sliding through it.

## How it's drawn

Technically a fold is four clipping planes applied to every material inside the scroll area. Nested scroll areas intersect their planes. See [[World Layout]].
