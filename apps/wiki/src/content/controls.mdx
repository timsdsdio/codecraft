| Action | Desktop | Touch |
|---|---|---|
| Move | <Key>W</Key><Key>A</Key><Key>S</Key><Key>D</Key> | left-thumb joystick |
| Look | mouse (pointer lock) | right-thumb drag |
| Jump / up a floor | <Key>Space</Key> | Jump button |
| Crouch / down a floor | <Key>Shift</Key> | Crouch button |
| Straight to the screen | <Key>Home</Key> | long-press Jump |
| Sprint | <Key>Ctrl</Key> | push the joystick to the edge |
| Use the app (click) | left click on the crosshair | tap the screen |
| Inspect | look at something | tap a machine |
| Stop typing | <Key>Esc</Key> | tap outside the input |

Crouch is <Key>Shift</Key>, as in Minecraft, so sprint moves to <Key>Ctrl</Key>.

## Using the app while small

Clicking goes to the app element under your crosshair. The game works out where the crosshair hits the screen and sends the app a real click there ([[Screen Rendering]]). Clicking an input focuses it. From then on, your keys type into the app instead of moving you, until you press <Key>Esc</Key> or click away.

On touch, taps on the screen go to the app wherever you tap. Taps on machines inspect them.
