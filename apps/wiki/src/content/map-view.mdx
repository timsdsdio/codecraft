The **Map View** is a bird's-eye camera over the whole room. First person is for *being* in the machine; the map is for *seeing* it. Press <Key>M</Key> to switch. Your position is marked on the floor.

| Action | Desktop | Touch |
|---|---|---|
| Wire | click a socket, then a matching one | tap, then tap |
| Move a block | press and drag | long-press, then drag |
| Orbit / zoom | drag / wheel | one finger / pinch |
| Jump to a zone | <Key>0</Key>–<Key>4</Key> | focus buttons |
| Inspect | side panel | bottom sheet |
| Delete | <Key>Delete</Key> | Delete button |

Wiring is tap-then-tap here too: dragging a cable would fight the camera on a phone.
