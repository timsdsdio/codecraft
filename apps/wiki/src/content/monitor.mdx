The **monitor** is where the app runs. It stands at the front of the room, its screen facing away from the blocks and its back facing them. You wire the back of the monitor and walk around to look at the front.

## Root socket

The back carries one large female socket for <Iface id="ui.Root@1" female />. Whatever is plugged in there is the app. In the starter set that's the [[App Shell]].

The monitor is where [[Composition]] starts: only blocks that the root socket depends on run.

## The screen

- **Live** (cyan glow): the app is running and you can use it. Aim the [[Probe]] at the screen to open it full size.
- **Missing** (red glow): something on the chain is unplugged. The screen lists what's missing.

<SceneFigure name="monitor-missing" float="right" width={300} caption="Nothing in the root socket: the screen goes red." />

The monitor is bolted down. The [[Wrench]] refuses it.
