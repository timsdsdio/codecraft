The **Wire Coil** is a spool of cable on a fork handle. It runs [[Cable|cables]].

## Running a cable

1. Use it on a [[Socket]]. The cable's loose end now hangs from that socket to your hand, in the interface's colour.
2. Every socket that could take the cable pulses brightly. Everything else dims. The room itself shows you where you can go.
3. Use it on one of the pulsing sockets. The cable snaps in with a flash.

You can start from either end, an output or an input. If the target input already holds its one cable, the new cable replaces it.

## Why two clicks

Both the first-person view and the [[Map View]] wire by "this, then that" rather than by dragging. Two short actions are forgiving, and they work the same with a mouse, a thumb, or a controller.
