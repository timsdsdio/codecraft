A **state tank** is one `useState` (or `useReducer`) hook. Tanks stand in a row beside their component's [[Logic Casing]], in the order the hooks are called.

## Reading a tank

- **Colour**: the value's [[Value Kinds|kind]].
- **Label**: the hook's name and a short preview of its value, such as `todos · Array(10)`.
- **Level**: how full it is, as a rough feel only. For arrays, the level follows the length. For other values the level doesn't mean anything; the tank just sloshes when its value changes.

## When state changes

An [[Event Wire]] pulse arrives, the fluid sloshes and settles at its new level, and the casing [[Render|renders]]. Every change in the factory starts at a tank.

## Names

React doesn't record hook variable names. The [[Probe]] reads them from source maps when they're available (as React DevTools does). Otherwise the tank is labelled by position (`state 1`, `state 2`).
