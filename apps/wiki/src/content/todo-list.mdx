The **Todo List** renders the todos that pass the current filter. It takes the todos model and the filter model on its back, and a row view in the slot on its top. It provides a <Iface id="ui.View@1" /> from its front.

## Swapping the row

The row isn't built into the list. It's whatever is plugged into the `item` slot on top: [[Todo Item]] in the starter world, or the alternative [[neon-todo-item|Neon Item]]. Plug the other one in and the old cable is replaced; the monitor changes straight away.

<SceneFigure name="replace" float="none" height={280} caption="Todo Item is plugged in. Neon Item is idle beside it, and its plug pulses as a valid replacement." />

With nothing in the slot, the list can't render and the monitor shows what's missing (see [[Composition]]).
