A **display link** is the last step of the journey: a thin beam from the top of a [[Logic Casing]] straight up to the underside of the [[Screen]]. Where it arrives, the component's box is outlined.

The name is borrowed from Create, where a Display Link sends information to a display board.

## Behaviour

- It flares whenever its component [[Render|renders]].
- It passes straight through any floors in between. A shallow component like `Header` has a long link; a list row has a short one.
- A component whose own JSX paints nothing (a pure layout wrapper like `App`'s `main`) has no link.
- Inside a scroll area, links are clipped by the [[Fold]] along with everything else.
