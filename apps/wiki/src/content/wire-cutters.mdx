The **Wire Cutters** remove [[Cable|cables]].

| Aimed at | Result |
|---|---|
| a cable | cuts it |
| a [[Socket]] | cuts every cable plugged into it ("Nothing to cut" if it's empty) |

Cutting a cable on the active chain usually breaks the app; the [[Monitor]] immediately shows what's missing. That's often the point: unplug a part, see what depends on it.
