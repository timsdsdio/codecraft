The **screen** is the app itself, rendered for real and lying face up. It's the roof of the factory and the ground you start on.

## Scale

You are 100 CSS px tall. The reference app is 400 px wide, so it's four of you across: about the size of a large room. A list row is about half your height. Body text is about the height of your shoe. You walk over the UI the way you'd walk over a board game the size of a floor.

## Using the app

The screen isn't a picture of the app; it *is* the app. Aim at a button and click to press it. Click an input to type into it. While you're typing, movement keys go to the input, and <Key>Esc</Key> gives them back to you. See [[Controls]] and [[Screen Rendering]].

## From below

From inside the factory you see the screen's underside. Each component's patch of screen is outlined there by its [[Display Link]], so looking up shows the screen divided into the components that draw it.
