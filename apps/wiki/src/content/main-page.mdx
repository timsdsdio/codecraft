**CodeCraft** shrinks you down to 100 pixels tall and drops you onto a running React app. The app's screen lies face-up on the ground, and you stand on it, on top of the buttons and list rows. **Crouch** and you drop through the screen into the factory underneath: the machinery that makes the app. Brass pipes carry props up to each part of the screen, green [[Logic Casing|logic casings]] turn props into output, and [[Telescoping Piston|telescoping pistons]] push out one arm for every item in a list. **Jump** to go back up.

It plays like the *Create* mod for Minecraft (tooltips, goggles, machines you can walk around), but much smaller, and in the neon look of the original prototype. There's a handful of machines, all of them there to show what React is doing.
