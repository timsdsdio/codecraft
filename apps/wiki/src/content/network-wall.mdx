The **network wall** separates the server [[Zone]] from the client zone. It stands across the middle of the room, glowing and translucent.

## Crossing it

- **You** can walk through either of its two doorways.
- **A cable** can't. The only way through is a link block, which sits in the portal in the middle of the wall and carries a [[Cable#Network cables|network cable]] from one side to the other.

That's the wall enforcing itself: client code can't accidentally hold a reference into the server.

The wall is 4.6 m tall. Standing on top of a [[Block]] you can see over it.
