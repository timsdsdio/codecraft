The room is divided into **zones**, one per tier of the app. Every [[Block]] belongs to one zone, and can't be set down outside it. A browser-only block can't wander into the server.

| Zone | Colour | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| <ZoneTag zone="client" /> | cyan | UI, client state, views |
| <ZoneTag zone="server" /> | amber | services, repositories, clocks |
| <ZoneTag zone="link" /> | magenta | link blocks, inside the wall's portal |
| <ZoneTag zone="db" /> | grey | storage (not built yet: [[Database Zone]]) |

## Layout

From back to front: database, server, the [[Network Wall]], client, and the [[Monitor]] at the very front. Data flows forward, so a cable never needs to run backwards.

Zone names are painted on the floor in the zone's colour. The floor grid shifts colour at the wall.
